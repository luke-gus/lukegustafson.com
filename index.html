Luke's Leadership Briefing is a concise email on Tennessee politics and news, put together by Luke Gustafson for a small group of readers.

Each edition pulls together the day's most important developments, with links to the original reporting and short notes on why they matter.

This is a request, not an instant signup. The briefing goes to a limited list, so Luke reviews every request personally. Submitting the form does not add you to the list. If your request is approved, you will hear from briefing@lukegustafson.com.
